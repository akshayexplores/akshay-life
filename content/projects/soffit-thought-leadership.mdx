Positioned Soffit as a credible voice in cybersecurity through systematic content and relationship architecture.

## The context

Soffit had real expertise in cybersecurity but a quiet voice in a noisy, fear-driven market.

## What I built

I built a systematic thought-leadership engine — a content cadence anchored in genuine point of view, paired with a relationship architecture that put that POV in front of the right people.

## What I learned

Credibility in B2B isn't bought with volume. It's earned by saying something true, consistently, to people who can verify it. Systems make consistency possible; honesty makes it land.
