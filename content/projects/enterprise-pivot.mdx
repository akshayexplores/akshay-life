Built an automated prospecting and outreach engine that replaced hours of manual SDR work with systematic, triggered sequences.

## The context

The team was spending hours on manual prospecting — research, list-building, follow-up — that scaled linearly with headcount.

## What I built

I built an automated prospecting engine: triggered sequences, enrichment, and follow-up logic stitched together so the repetitive work ran itself. SDRs got their time back for the parts only humans can do.

## What I learned

Automation earns its keep when it removes the work that shouldn't have needed a human in the first place — and protects the work that should.
