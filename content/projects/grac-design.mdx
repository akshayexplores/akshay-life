Comprehensive UI/UX design across the full GRAC product — from information architecture to final pixel.

## The context

GRAC needed a coherent product experience — not a coat of paint, but structure from the ground up.

## What I built

I designed across the full surface: information architecture, flows, interaction design, and the final pixel. Every screen answered to the same logic.

## What I learned

Good design is mostly good decisions made early. The pixels are downstream of whether the structure is honest about what the user is actually trying to do.
