Supported GRAC's fundraise with materials, positioning narrative, and investor-readiness strategy.

## The context

GRAC was heading into a raise and needed the story, the materials, and the readiness to match the substance of the business.

## What I built

I supported the fundraise end to end — sharpening the positioning narrative, building the materials, and getting the team investor-ready so the conversations stayed about the business, not the deck.

## What I learned

Investors fund clarity. The work of a fundraise is mostly the work of making something genuinely good legible to someone seeing it for the first time.
