Built the internal operating system that runs FastrBuild — task management, client workflows, team accountability, all without production code.

## The context

FastrBuild was scaling on goodwill and group chats. Work got done, but the system holding it together lived in people's heads.

## What I built

I built the operating layer in Coda — a single source of truth for tasks, client workflows, ownership, and status. Every handoff became visible. Every commitment had a name attached.

## What I learned

A company doesn't need software to have an operating system. It needs clarity about who does what, by when, and what 'done' means. Tools just make that clarity durable.
